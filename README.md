## About me

I build products that solve real problems in health, finance, agriculture and civic tech across Africa. I work at [Technetium Kenya](https://www.technetium.co.ke/) and I'm based in Nairobi, Kenya.

- I build with Web2, Web3, UI/UX, data and AI
- I love hackathons and have taken first place at many of them with my teams
- I speak about turning coursework into real skills and careers

## Things I've built

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Campaign Finance Watch Tool](https://campaignwatch.tikenya.org/) | Open data platform that tracks political campaign spending in Kenya |
| [SkillsSync Global](https://www.skillssync.xyz/) | AI engine that turns coursework and project files into verified career readiness evidence |
| [MediTrust](https://meditrust.medibora.com/) | Claims validation and payments for primary care, built on openIMIS with fraud detection |
| [MediBora Health](https://www.medibora.com/) | Digital health products, including the Tutela wearable safety device |
| [TradeLink Africa](https://www.tradelinkafrica.xyz/) | Agri fintech and B2B exchange for African traders |
